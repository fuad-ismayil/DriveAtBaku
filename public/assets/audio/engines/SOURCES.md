# Engine recording sources

The engine system crossfades these recordings by simulated RPM and throttle/load. The copies in this directory are unmodified WAV source files; loop-edge smoothing and level matching happen when they are decoded in the game.

- `car-rpm-0.wav` through `car-rpm-5.wav`: “racing car engine sound loops” by domasx2, [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/racing-car-engine-sound-loops), CC0. Original source filenames: `loop_0.wav`, `loop_1_0.wav`, `loop_2_0.wav`, `loop_3_0.wav`, `loop_4_0.wav`, `loop_5_0.wav`.
- `v8-load-5570.wav` and `v8-overrun-5580.wav`: “Generic V8 Engine Sound” by DerMeehdrescher / Meehdrescher Studios, [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/generic-v8-engine-sound), CC BY-SA 4.0. Original source filenames: `Acc_05570.wav` and `Dec_05581.wav`. Credit: Sound made by DerMeehdrescher / Meehdrescher Studios.
